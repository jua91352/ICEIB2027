Figures for the FMPA paper.

- `architecture.tex` (Fig. 1) and `flowchart.tex` (Fig. 2) are TikZ drawings, included with `\input{figures/...}` and compiled by pdfLaTeX on Overleaf.
- To use an exported diagram instead (e.g., draw.io -> PDF), place `architecture.pdf` here and replace the `\input` with `\includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{architecture.pdf}`.
